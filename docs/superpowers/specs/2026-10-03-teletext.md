# Teletext WebVTT stage

The user needs European and North American subtitles selectable in HLS, original pass-through or filtering, friendly Stream/Template controls, and an independent server. This stage adds selected DVB teletext pages to the existing608/708 conversion pipeline. It is not bitmap OCR or full regional parity.

## Architecture and contract

Implement independent bounded Rust Level1 page decoding. Keep a single FFmpeg input session: its private copied video output also copies subtitle PES when teletext is selected. No official Flussonic dependency or source media, and no upstream decoder code in the product. Reuse authorized TS/fMP4 WebVTT delivery and AV-fallback lifecycle.

`flussonix_hls_captions` rows accept exactly one of channel1..4, service1..63 or teletext_page100..899 plus existing language/name. Four total distinct renditions. Existing wire fields/URLs remain unchanged. Internally widen Service/Cue identity to u16: page identity1024+page, keys ttx100..ttx899. Digital65..127 and analog1..4 stay stable. Friendly format/page fields on existing rows; template inheritance and policy independence unchanged.

Decode announced teletext descriptors0x56 in MPEG-TS private-stream PID(s), match configured pages, and retain a maximum of four matching PIDs. Ambiguous duplicate page announcements on distinct PIDs fail that page rather than selecting randomly. PMT changes and discontinuities clear affected page state. Bounded PSI4096 bytes, PES65541 bytes, complete framed DVB data units44bytes; check CRC, continuity, scrambling, transport errors and timestamps. Data units0x02/0x03, bit reversal, Hamming8/4 correction, odd character parity. A malformed teletext PID degrades captions and clears its pages without clearing608/708 or stopping AV. Missing teletext pages report explicitly; no claim of automatic discovery.

Selected pages retain24x40 rows. Support header/serial and parallel magazine association, erase/inhibit/subpage changes, Latin G0 and default Western/Central European national subsets (English, German, Swedish/Finnish/Hungarian, Italian, French, Portuguese/Spanish, Czech/Slovak). Render boxed subtitle text, suppress concealed and mosaic content, flatten styling/height. Ignore links and service data. X/26/X/28/M/29 enhancement and alternate character sets are explicitly unsupported and degrade/clear affected output, avoiding silently mislabeled text.

A page transaction begins at header PTS, completes on a terminating different-page header or after100ms of source-clock inactivity; commit text at the transaction's header PTS, with unchanged retransmissions deduplicated. Erase and blank pages close cues, no wall-clock subtitle expiry. Source video provides the same source anchor and safe DTS frontier; process completed teletext PES before advancing that frontier, including33-bit wrap and B-frame reorder. Queue at most64 PES events/256KiB; source gaps reset clocks/pages alongside existing captions. No early freezing of incomplete page updates.

## Evidence and limits

Own literal packet fixtures cover page isolation, header flags/characters, corrupt parity/Hamming and continuity, interleaved magazines, partial updates, silence, wrap, PMT replacement and bounds. Generate H264/AAC media with two teletext pages, explicit boxed German/French text, update and erase, and unrelated DVB tracks. Copy/CPU real worker tests prove both HLS variants, source cue times, silence, original-track independence and auth/revocation. Compare words against independent FFmpeg/libzvbi in private research, not a production/test dependency. Browser playback must read external WebVTT, not embedded caption fallback. GPU, non-Latin/enhanced pages, native separate subtitle relay and real HEVC player delivery remain unqualified; DVB OCR follows separately.

Use the established isolated inline workflow, one fresh final reviewer, one RED→GREEN fix pass, complete suites, exact-head successful CI before authorized FF main publication; restore the existing local preview only after verification and archive private evidence before normal worktree cleanup.
