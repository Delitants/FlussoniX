# RTSP push implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans inline and test-driven-development, followed by one fresh whole-branch review.

**Goal:** Push shared stream media to RTSP/RTSPS receivers with verified TLS and friendly configuration.

**Architecture:** A mixed destination adapter preserves SRT and wraps a new native RTSP supervisor. A bounded local bridge owns verified upstream sockets, request authority translation, negotiated channels and genuine RTP progress. Worker ownership remains unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** Rust/Tokio/Rustls, independent FFmpeg, React/Playwright.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-rtsp-push.md

## Global Constraints

- Four total SRT/RTSP/RTSPS destinations; timeouts/URL limits and options exactly as in spec.
- No vendor components, production changes, secret diagnostics, insecure TLS or second encoding.
- All reader/bridge tasks joined on cancellation; preserve preview config/credentials and evidence.
- Final exact-head complete CI before main integration/release.

## Review Focus

- Destination query/path substitution and redirects must never change upstream identity or leak credentials.
- A forged/mismatched RECORD response or RTCP-only traffic cannot claim media delivery or mask stalls.
- Negotiated channels must match TCP offers and cannot collide or introduce UDP after setup.
- Cancellation during DNS/TLS/setup/write must join readers and close all bridge sockets before retries/replacement.
- Mixed template destinations retain source order and never transfer to discovered mirrors; friendly controls preserve secrets while masking summaries.
