## Review Focus

- Sequence discontinuity, partial packet/token, malformed extended blocks and nonselected-service data must not leave stale text or desynchronize 608/AV.
- Delay cancellation/reset and silent-video clock advancement must preserve source-time boundaries and bounded memory.
- Dynamic service asset names and wire serialization must not leak internal IDs, collide with CC identities or bypass auth.
- Window resizing/directions/Unicode and adversarial input must remain bounded; qualified plaintext limitations must be candid.
- Worker failure/replacement, wrap/reorder, HLS retention and browser in-band fallback must not manufacture misleading playback proof.
